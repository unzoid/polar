## Polarity is a player

Polarity plays media and keeps your library. It doesn't ship, host or suggest any content, and it doesn't provide,
index or recommend content sources.

Add-ons are software made by other people, which you choose to install. You're responsible for the add-ons you
install and for what you play with them: only use Polarity for content you're entitled to watch.

## Torrent playback

When you play a torrent source, Polarity downloads it from other people and uploads parts of it to them while it
plays, up to the upload limit in Settings → Sources. If you turn on Keep sharing after playback, it goes on uploading
until you quit Polarity. The other people in a torrent can see your IP address.

## Accounts

You can use Polarity without an account. An account syncs your profiles, library, watch progress, playlists and
settings between your devices.

- **Limits:** an account has up to 5 profiles; each profile up to 5,000 library titles, 5,000 titles with watch
  progress, 5,000 anime links and 100 playlists; an account up to 10,000 library titles and 10,000 titles with watch
  progress. When a part is full, the app says so.
- **Misuse** includes: using the account service to store anything other than Polarity's own data; reaching it other
  than through the app, or automating it to send far more than the app would; trying to get into an account that
  isn't yours, or around its limits; and sharing links in watch parties to content you aren't entitled to share, or
  disrupting other people's parties.
- Accounts used for misuse can be suspended or removed. You can delete your own account at any time in Settings →
  Account → Delete account.
